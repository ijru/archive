AJRU — Asian Jump Rope Union

About

AJRU was founded 2019 by representatives from the former Asian Rope Skipping Federation (ARSF) and the Asian Pacific Jump Rope Federation (APJRF). More information can be found on their website ajru.info

Web Presence

AJRU has been accessible via https://ajru.sport since its start.

Competitions

As of May 2026, AJRU has held or scheduled the following competitions

  1. 2022—AJRU Virtual Championships—Virtual/Digital
  2. 2024—Asian Jump Rope Championships—Kawasaki, Japan
  3. 2026—Asian Jump Rope Championships—Shangyu, China
